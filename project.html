PROJECT OVERVIEW: Plant Diseases

This initiative was developed as part of our college Community Service Project (CSP).

We selected this topic because plant health directly impacts global food security and local agricultural economies. Through this project, we learned how to identify pathogens and understand environmental triggers.

By creating awareness, we empower farmers and home gardeners to adopt sustainable prevention methods, ensuring a greener future.
